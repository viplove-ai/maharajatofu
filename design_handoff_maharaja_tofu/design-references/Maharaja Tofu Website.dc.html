KEYBOARD APPEARS TWICE
Phone and pincode only — inputmode="numeric" on both. Everything else is a stepper, slider, chip or native select. Name is the one text field and it is first-name-only, four letters for most people.

The counter shows a whole number, the capacity behind it (60 kg a week), who last updated it and when. No seconds ticking, no midnight reset, no red flashing. It moves down only after a batch is counted — which is the one thing a fake counter never does.
